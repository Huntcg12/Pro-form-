Pro Form

Margin ratings + FormFav form → win chances → fair prices

Get started

  1. Settings: paste your FormFav API key. It is saved in this browser only.
  2. History: sync the last 7 days (all FormFav allows). After that the app tops up each time you open it, and the ratings sharpen as history grows.
  3. Race card: pick a meeting and race, enter the prices you can get, and look for flagged edges.

Edges only appear once every runner has a price, because they come from the model combined with the market. The model on its own disagrees with the market most on long shots, and in testing those bets lost heavily. Enter the price you can actually get in the Market column. Pro data has no live odds or scratchings, so tick Scr for late scratchings. Probabilities recalculate for the remaining field.

Backtest

Each race is scored using only what was known before it: margin ratings from earlier runs, plus FormFav's pre-race form. Factor weights are fitted on the earliest 75% of races and tested on the latest 25%, which the fit never sees. Lower log-loss is better.

A bet is struck when model chance × SP − 1 reaches the band. SP is the final market, so beating it is a hard test. Treat small samples as noise.

Results history

FormFav only serves the last 7 days, so history has to be built going forward. The app tops up automatically each time you open it; if a week passes without opening it, the missed days can't be fetched later. Each day costs about 140 requests (results plus pre-race form). Keep the app open while it syncs.
